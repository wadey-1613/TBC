Goal: march up the table and destroy the enemy castle (king). Yours is at the bottom.
Each turn: you are offered 3 infantry: deploy one (free), recycle one to the bottom of the deck, the third is discarded. When a deck runs out, its discard pile is shuffled back in. You are offered 2 squad cards: play one or hold it (hand max 3), the other is discarded. You can play 1 squad card a turn. Move up to 3 units a step each (forward or sideways), but only units that were on the board when your turn began. Every unit may attack once. New cards appear on your back row.
Card stats: ⚔ attack · 🛡 armour (cuts every hit) · ♥ health (now/max).
Reach shapes (up is toward the enemy castle):

A unit strikes back only if the attacker is adjacent and inside its own shape. ♦ Siege ignores armour. Damage boosts only hit enemies one of your units could attack. Select a unit to see its reach. Right-click / Esc cancels.
