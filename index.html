How to play
Click a hand card, then a lane (and a target if needed). Click a green-glow minion to attack or shift lanes. Minions hit the enemy in their own or adjacent lanes; an empty opposing lane lets them hit the hero. Right-click / Esc cancels.
Swift: acts when played. Ward: blocks one hit. Ranged: any lane, no retaliation.
