Goal: march up the table and destroy the enemy castle (king). Yours is at the bottom. If nobody does, the player who owns more tiles when the rounds run out wins (a tie goes to the healthier castle).
Land: the board is 9 rows. Rows 1-4 start as yours (blue), rows 6-9 as theirs (red), row 5 is neutral. Stepping on a tile claims it, and it stays yours after you leave until an enemy steps on it. Hidden rogues claim nothing. The ♣ Land Grab card claims a 3x3 area next to your land (not tiles holding enemy units).
Outposts ⚑: four marked tiles. Hold one with a unit for a full turn, then build a 🏹 Lookout (a ranged unit standing there shoots one tile further), a ⛺ Bunker (+1 infantry each turn, and your infantry can be placed around it) or a ⛏ Gold Mine (+1 mana each turn). They work for whoever is standing on them.
Legend ★: your king can summon a Legend (the button above your hand). It moves up to 3 tiles a turn, claims every tile it lands on, and is about twice as tough as a normal unit. The more land you own the higher its level, and the more mana it costs. Only one at a time: after it falls you wait as many turns as it cost.
Each turn: you draw 1 squad card (hand up to 8; you start with 5) and get 1 free infantry to place straight away. Mana is 1 on your first turn, 2 on your second, up to 10: spend it to play as many cards from your hand as you can afford (the number in the corner is the cost). Spend 1 mana once a turn to swap the free infantry for the next one. An unplaced infantry, and any card drawn into a full hand, goes to the bottom of its deck. When a deck runs out, its discard pile is shuffled back in.
Move up to 3 units a step each (forward or sideways), but only units on the board when your turn began. Every unit may attack once. New cards appear on your back row. Defence units (▣ and ♠) can also go anywhere within 2 tiles of your king.
Rogues 🗡 start hidden: they can't be targeted. A hidden rogue moves 2 tiles in any direction and has one special action, which reveals it for good: lay a trap (☠ Poison 1 damage on each of its next 2 turns · 🕸 Sticky can't move next turn · ✦ Flash can't attack next turn) on an empty tile next to it, throw a 💣 bomb (exactly 2 tiles away, 1 damage to that tile and every tile around it, friends too, ignores armour), or make a sneak attack for double damage. After that it is a plain melee unit. Enemies that step on a trap spring it.
Stats: ⚔ attack · 🛡 armour plates · ♥ health. Armour is a pool of plates: a normal hit removes half its strength (rounded up) from the plates and leaves health alone; once they're gone, hits go to health at full strength. Siege (♦ breach), poison and bombs ignore armour.
Walls (♠ cards, the ♦ Siege Tower and ▣ defence infantry) can't attack and take double damage from behind. ▣ Defence infantry counter a melee hit from the front or sides for half its strength (if they survive); ♠ walls never counter. A Siege Tower holds one archer: it shoots from inside, can't be targeted, and drops out if the tower falls.
